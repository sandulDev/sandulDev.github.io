Statok — Money Tracker

Expenses, limits, payments and other people's money. Offline, no ads, no account.

Features

  • Add an expense in seconds: amount, tag, done. There is a home-screen widget, a Quick Settings tile and an app shortcut.
  • Monthly limits for tags: what is left and whether you are over. Your month can start on any day, for example the 25th, when your salary arrives.
  • Other people's money, kept separate: money that belongs to a company, relatives or friends goes in a separate budget, and you see how much of the money on your card is not yours and who owes whom.
  • Recurring payments with reminders; the "Paid" button is right in the notification.
  • Installments on a credit card: schedule, what is left, and the last payment.
  • Monthly statistics: income, spending and what is left, and where the money went by tag.
  • Search your whole history, PIN or biometric lock, backup to a file, CSV export, dark theme.

Privacy

All data stays on your phone only. There is no account, no ads, and the app has no internet access and collects no data.
